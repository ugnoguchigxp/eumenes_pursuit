# Fixes 2026-10-22 (i): hz11m follow-ups

## §5 Targets are honest per hypothesis, not maximal
hz11m: every hypothesis declared targets up front and no step back happened, but one hypothesis
(sales-pipeline) declared seven directories, five of which do not exist in the task (src/, scripts/, lib/,
bin/, pipeline/). The spec-h sentence 「直すつもりがあるなら、最初から targets に書いておくと区切りが要りません」
rewards broad declarations, and broad targets hide the scope-growth signal. New wording:
「直すつもりがあるなら、この仮説で実際に変えるファイルかディレクトリだけを最初から targets に書けば区切りは要りません。
確かめていない場所や「念のため」の場所は書かないでください（広く書くほど、範囲の拡大という兆候が見えなくなります）。」
No mechanical check of target existence (the engine does not own the task filesystem); the evidence script counts
hypotheses whose targets name paths absent from the task, as a monitor.

Items 1–4 of the hz11m list are harness/docs changes (private run dirs, eval task README, private e2e replay);
see the eval branch and the private CHANGES.
